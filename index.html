Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Flipspark is built to run entirely on your device. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your information (very little) and how to get help.

Privacy Policy

What we collect: nothing

Flipspark does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third party tracking of any kind.

Your content stays on your device

Anything you create or change in the app, such as your custom prompts and your settings, is saved only on your device. It is shared with the Flipspark widget on that same device so your daily prompt stays in sync. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Feedback email

If you tap "Send feedback" in Settings, the app opens an email addressed to us. Sending it is optional and fully in your control. If you do send it, we receive whatever you write, plus a short footer showing your app version, device model, and iOS version, which helps us track down problems. We use this only to reply to you and to improve the app, and we do not share it.

Children

Flipspark does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date shown above.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email support@altroadstudio.com.

Support

How to play

Flipspark hands you creative music production prompts. Shuffle for a full four suit combo, or pick a single suit to draw from. You can add your own custom prompts too. Add the Flipspark widget to your home screen for a fresh prompt each day.

Get in touch

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email support@altroadstudio.com, or tap "Send feedback" inside the app under Settings. We read everything.
